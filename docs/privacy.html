ilm.

Privacy

Short version: Ilm collects nothing about you.

What it does

Ilm reads the Google Classroom page you have open to find the files attached to it, then asks your browser to download them to your device. That is all.

Data

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third-party server. There is no server.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.
  • The only network requests go to Google (classroom.google.com, drive.google.com, docs.google.com and Google's file-download hosts), using your own Google login, to fetch your own files.
  • Files are saved to your device's Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded or saved to Google Drive.
  • Your choices (folder naming, PDF or Office export) are stored only in your browser on your device.

Why it asks for permissions

  • Access to Google sites: to read the Classroom page and to fetch the files you ask for.
  • Downloads: to save the files to your device.
  • Scripting: to read the attachments on the page you have open.
  • Storage: to remember progress while the popup is closed, and your choices.

Respecting owners

If a file's owner has disabled downloading, Ilm does not bypass that. The file is listed as skipped.

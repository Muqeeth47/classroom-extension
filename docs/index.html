ilm.

Every file from your class. One click.

Download every PDF, document and attachment from Google Classroom. Original formats. No zip. Saved to your device.

Free and open source (MIT). Not affiliated with Google. Privacy · Licences

Download

Download .zip

Then follow the to install it.

Manual

For desktop browsers: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Firefox.

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin ilm. from the puzzle-piece menu.

Firefox: about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox → Load Temporary Add-on → pick manifest.json, then press Allow access.

Use

  1. Open Google Classroom, then open the announcement or post whose files you want. (For a whole class, open its Classwork tab.)
  2. Click ilm. and press Get files from this class (or this post).
  3. Untick anything you don't want. Rename the folder if you like.
  4. Press Download. Files land in Downloads/Classroom/ on your computer.

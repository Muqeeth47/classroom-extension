Classroom Bulk Downloader

Every file from a Google Classroom class, in one click.

Saves PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images and more to your computer, in their original format. No zip files.

Install

Works on Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Firefox, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

  1. Click Download above and unzip the file.
  2. Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera: open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder, the one that contains manifest.json.
  4. Pin the extension from the puzzle-piece menu so it stays visible.

Firefox

  1. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  2. Click Load Temporary Add-on and choose manifest.json from the unzipped folder.
  3. Open the extension and press Allow access when asked. Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it restarts, so repeat this after a restart.

How to use

  1. Open Google Classroom, open a class, and go to its Classwork tab.
  2. Click the extension and press Find files in this class. It reads every post and any Drive folders attached to them.
  3. Untick anything you don't want. You can rename the folder and choose whether to sort files by topic.
  4. Press Download. Files appear in your Downloads/Classroom/ folder, on this device. Nothing is uploaded or saved to Google Drive.

Good to know

  • Original formats. Uploaded files are saved exactly as they are. Google Docs, Slides and Sheets have no original file, so you choose PDF or Word / PowerPoint / Excel.
  • Private. It runs in your browser with your own Google login. It collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere except to Google to fetch your files.
  • Teacher restrictions are respected. If a file's owner turned off downloading, it is listed as skipped.
  • School accounts. If your school admin blocks extensions or Drive downloads, it won't work for you.
  • Safari isn't supported yet.
